Basic information

Parallel Events are a type of event in Crush Crush, giving players a chance to unlock new characters and special outfits, including DX wedding dresses which were previously given as a reward for purchasing diamonds.

How do Parallel Events work?

Parallel Events work the same as the base game, except that there is no resetting, and you only have a limited time to complete it.  
  
Each event has a set of goals you need to complete, which give you either rewards for the event itself or for the main game. The final reward of each Parallel Event is a brand new girl for the main game, only available by completing the event.  
  
Goals that you will see include:  

* Job Progress
* Hobby Progress
* Money Earned
* Relationship Progress
* Gilding Jobs or Hobbies

  
Rather than diamonds, you earn a special Event Currency which you can spend on gilding, speed boosts, time skips, etc. If you wish, you can trade diamonds from your main game for additional Event Currency. Any remaining Event Currency is lost when the event ends, so don't trade more than you need to.

What do I get for playing the events?

Starting with Yuki's November 2025 rerun on Steam and Kyoko's March 2026 rerun everywhere else, the events no longer award fixed prizes - instead you'll earn tickets, which you can redeem at the ticket booth for a prize of your choice.   
  
You can trade in the tickets for outfits, pin-ups, and girls from Parallel Events. Outfits cost 30 tickets, pin-ups 60 tickets. A girl is 100 tickets if she's currently featured in an event, or 500 tickets otherwise. Playing through an event will give you enough tickets to get the same rewards as before. If you don't already own the featured girl, you will receive her when you complete the event; if you already own her, you'll receive 100 tickets instead.  
  
This means there's more reason to play the reruns - you can save up for a future reward if you already have the featured girl, so you don't have to miss out if there's an event you can't play. There are also some special, ticket booth-only rewards, such as an exclusive pin-up, to give you a long-term goal to save up for.  
  
The Fuzzy Festival with Ginger and Wasabi, which is used as a newcomer event, will continue to have fixed prizes, since it runs differently from other Parallel Events.

Gilding explained

**What is gilding?**  
   
 Gilding is done by using Event Currency to increase the speed at which a hobby is trained, or   
 increase the money you earn whenever you complete a job.  
  
**Is gilding present in the main game?**   
   
Yes, gilding is also present as a main game mechanic.  
  
**How do i gild a job/hobby?**  
   
Next to a job/hobby you will see a dimmed icon of the Event Currency.

Speed Boost explained

**What is a Speed Boost?**   
   
Speed Boosts speed up, well - everything! Hobbies, jobs, dates, and cooldowns for talking and gifting all get sped up by increasing your Speed Boost.  
  
**How do I get a speed boost?**  
  
There are two types of Speed Boost available during Parallel Events:  

* Speed Boost bought in the Shop for Event Currency
* Speed Boost earned from the goal rewards

  
**Speed Boost from the Shop:**  
   
This Speed Boost is obtained by buying it in exchange for Event Currency in the Shop. Buying a boost multiplies your existing multiplier, so buying a x8 boost twice is equivalent to buying a x64 boost once (because 8 \* 8 = 64).  
In Parallel Events, this multiplier maxes out at x64.  
  
**How much does it cost?**  
  
Speed Boosts are sold in 3 sizes: x2, x8 and x64. The cheapest way to maximize the boost is to buy the x64 once.  
  
However, if you're trying to complete the event without spending diamonds or cash, it may not be feasible to acquire enough event currency without any boost. Buying the x8 boost twice allows you to make more incremental progress at minimal extra cost.  
  
Do not buy the x2, as it is significantly more expensive overall compared to saving up. You should only buy the x2 if you have bought both the $4 event bundle and a x8 multiplier.  
  
**Where can i find it?**   
  
You can find the Speed Boosts In the shop under the Boosts tab, next to the Time Skips.  
  
**Speed Boost from event goal rewards**  
  
As you progress through the event, some goals will award Speed Boosts ("Goal Boosts" hereafter). This is a *different* multiplier from the ones bought from the store, and you will still need to buy those to maximize efficiency.  
  
**How do the two multipliers stack?**  
Just like the Store Boost and Reset Boost in the main game, Store Boost and Goal Boosts stack by multiplying each other. If you have x64 from Store Boosts and x16 from Goal Boosts (the maximum in the Frosty PES), that equates to a total speed multipler of x1024 - or to put it differently, something that would have taken a full day without the boost, will instead take about 85 seconds at maximum boost.

Can I complete the event without spending money or diamonds?

Yes. Parallel events are 100% possible to complete without spending any money or diamonds.  
  
**How do I do that?**   
  
You *will* need to put some time into the event, especially when the event starts. As you make more progress, you can go longer without checking in, but you do need to do so regularly.  
  
It is normally possible to complete the events this way in about 2/3 of the event time.  
For example, the first event - the Spooky Event - lasted 3 days, but could be completed in a little under 2. The next - the Frosty Event - lasted 5 days, but could be completed in about 3.5.  
  
**Okay, but what \*exactly\* should I do?**  
  
Each event is a little different, so you may need to vary your strategy a little from time to time.   
  
Generally speaking, however, your #1 priority is maxing out your Speed Boost from the shop, because you \*will\* need all of it. With no spending, this normally means you will want to buy the x8 multiplier twice, instead of waiting for enough Event Currency to buy the x64. However, there's no guarantee this will be the best option every time, so pay attention to the exact prices and plan based on when you get big Event Currency payouts from the goals.  
  
Pay close attention to the full list of goals. Goals must be completed in order, so you should focus on whatever your current goal is, but it's good to look ahead and try to prepare for future ones. In particular, make sure you've always got some Time Blocks allocated to hobbies that you might need, and note that unlike the main game, the hobbies take different amounts of time to train - so prioritize your Time Blocks accordingly and don't let anything fall too far behind.  
  
Although you don't need to put \*all\* of your Event Currency into the Speed Boosts, most of it will still go to that. You should have enough to gild a job or two; make sure you pick the most rewarding jobs for that. Gilding hobbies is also an option, but it's generally not been necessary.  
  
Avoid spending Event Currency on Time Skips unless you have maxed out your Speed Boost: you will get much more for your Event Currency once your Boost is maxed. You should also avoid buying more Time Blocks: although you may have to juggle them around a bit for each goal, the Goals provide all the Time Blocks you actually need to complete the event.  
  
When goals award Time Skips, you will need to claim them before you can progress. It's generally best to dedicate most of your time blocks to hobbies before claiming it, as well as one or two of your best-paying jobs.  
  
The Goals will generally not require you to bring everyone to Lover level, but it is still a good idea to do so anyway when you are able to do so: in addition to hobby levels, talking is also made significantly more effective the more girls you have at Lover level. This is particularly relevant at the end of the event, when you only have a few goals remaining.  
  
In short:  
  
Do:  

* Start the event as early as possible and check back regularly
* Prioritize the Speed Boost in the Shop over everything else
* Max out your Speed Boost
* Plan ahead if possible
* Pay close attention to the event

  
Do NOT:  

* Spend Event Currency on Time Blocks - you don't need them
* Gild hobbies unless you need to for a Goal
* Gild jobs unless you need to for a Goal, or you really need the extra income
* Buy Time Skips unless you are running out of time to complete the event
* Check in just once or twice a day - time is limited

What can I do if I'm busy during the event?

If you are busy during the event and can't commit the time needed to finish it your options are limited to just 2 :  
  
**Spend Diamonds:**  
This is an obvious one, but if you spend enough diamonds the events can be completed in as little as 20 minutes. Doing it that quickly will get quite expensive, so you'll want to make sure that you don't spend more than you have to - and if you don't need to be done quite that quickly, even smaller amounts of diamonds can end up making a very big difference.  
  
**Use Steam Link:**  
Steam Link is an App that allows you to stream games from your computer to your phone, tablet and TV and play them.  
  
**Link:**  
<https://store.steampowered.com/remoteplay#anywhere>

I spent diamonds/cash and still failed to complete the event!

It matters a lot what you spend your Event Currency on, and chances are you spent it wrongly.  
  
Even a small purchase - 40 diamonds or so, enough for an x8 boost - is enough to lower the amount of time it takes to complete the event \*dramatically\*.  
  
But just as for the F2P player, patience is key. An early Time Skip is worth very little in the long run, because once you max out your Speed Boost, that same amount of progress will take minutes, if not seconds.   
  
For that reason, you should \*always\* have the Speed Boost as your priority, even when spending resources.

I ran into a bug and can't complete/play the event anymore

**Currently known issues**  
  
If you're missing the event button, try restarting Steam and/or your computer.  
  
If you are missing some girls after switching back to the main game, they should return if you close the game and start it again.  
  
Even if you already own a reward from a goal, you will still need to meet the goal requirement and claim the goal again in order to proceed. This is true both for the final girl and any outfit rewards that are already marked with a checkmark.
